# HK position sources

Prefer HKEX filings, issuer results and guidance, placements, buybacks, major-holder actions, regulators, Stock Connect, and HKMA. Evaluate placement terms, mainland-policy linkage, A/H or ADR economics, US rates, and HKD liquidity without treating correlation as causation.

Search each security by five-digit code plus Chinese and English issuer names. Check HKEXnews and issuer investor-relations releases before general news. Establish the latest results, current outlook, placements or buybacks, major-holder changes, regulatory events, and next known reporting catalyst. For leveraged ETPs, prioritize product-provider factsheets, underlying-index moves, daily-reset mechanics, tracking, liquidity, premium-discount, and compounding decay.
