# US position sources

Prefer SEC filings, issuer releases, prepared remarks and calls, guidance, official agencies, and material competitor or supply-chain evidence. Distinguish company quality from surprise versus the priced baseline; disclose when consensus, valuation, or positioning evidence is unavailable.

Search order for each symbol: issuer investor-relations results and guidance; SEC 10-Q/10-K/8-K filings; earnings-call materials; then reputable reporting about material events. Verify the issuer identity because ticker-only searches can collide. Capture the latest reported quarter, changes to forward guidance, next known reporting catalyst, and the market expectation being tested. Do not invent price targets or stop levels from price history alone.
