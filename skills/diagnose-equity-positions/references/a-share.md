# A-share position sources

Prefer exchange and issuer announcements, results and previews, CSRC and ministry releases, disclosed shareholder changes, buybacks, unlocks, investigations, and official industry data. Compare policy transmission with issuer economics; do not assume a policy label becomes company earnings.

Search each security by six-digit code plus Chinese issuer name. Check SSE/SZSE issuer announcements and the issuer's official disclosures before general news. Establish the latest results or performance preview, management guidance when disclosed, material contracts, shareholder or executive actions, financing, investigations, unlocks, and the next known reporting event. For ETFs, use fund-manager documents and index methodology instead of treating the ETF as an operating company.
