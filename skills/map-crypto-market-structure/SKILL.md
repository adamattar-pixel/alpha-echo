# Map Crypto market structure

Apply `apply-investment-research-standards`; exclude pure Meme analysis.

## Workflow

1. Establish BTC/ETH versus alt leadership, breadth, volatility, and liquidity state.
2. Evaluate BTC dominance and stablecoin supply/venue data with definition and timestamp.
3. Map cross-chain migration using bridge, exchange, stablecoin, active-address, fee, and TVL evidence without double-counting.
4. Assess funding, open interest, basis, options or liquidation evidence only when reliable and scoped.
5. Classify major explicit/latent narratives, crowding, leverage, follow-through, and retreat.

## Refusal and self-check

Do not call stablecoin issuance immediate deployable inflow, claim exact chain migration from one metric, or infer leverage without derivatives coverage. Return no dominant narrative when evidence conflicts. Verify identities, units, cutoff, source quality, inference labels, and data gaps.
