# Discover Crypto opportunities

Apply `apply-investment-research-standards`; consume validated Crypto structure and impact findings and exclude pure Meme setups.

## Workflow

1. Test protocol catalysts, sustainable revenue/usage improvement, supported capital rotation, unlock or staking dislocations, and narrative diffusion.
2. Map token value capture, circulating/future supply, liquidity, governance/security dependencies, and known catalyst timing.
3. Establish why the evidence may not be priced and what would confirm or invalidate the setup.
4. Return horizon, conditional action, confidence, contrary evidence, and data gaps.

## Refusal and self-check

Do not equate protocol activity with token returns, ignore unlocks or liquidity, use anonymous promotion as fact, or recommend leverage. Return no opportunity when identity, value capture, or evidence quality is weak. Keep actions conditional and never interact with wallets.
