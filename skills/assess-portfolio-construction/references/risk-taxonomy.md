# Portfolio construction taxonomy

- **Position size**: exposure is disproportionate to thesis strength, loss severity, liquidity, or stated risk budget.
- **Industry/theme**: several holdings depend on the same demand cycle, narrative, or valuation regime.
- **Macro factor**: several holdings depend on growth, inflation, rates, liquidity, currency, policy, commodity, or geopolitics.
- **Economic identity**: different listings, share classes, ETFs, or suppliers reproduce one underlying exposure.
- **Currency/geography**: quote currency, revenue exposure, and listing venue create distinct risks.
- **Liquidity**: exit capacity is weak relative to position size or several exits may be needed together.
- **Catalyst calendar**: several holdings rely on or are threatened by the same event window.
- **Time horizon**: short-term and long-term intents compete for the same risk budget.
