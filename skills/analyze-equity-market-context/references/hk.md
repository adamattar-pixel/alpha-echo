# HK equity market context

Prefer HKEX, Stock Connect, HKMA, index providers, issuer disclosures, and official mainland policy sources.

Evaluate breadth, turnover, southbound preference, HKD liquidity, A/H and ADR linkages, sector leadership, crowding, and index-versus-stock money-making effects. Separate disclosed flow from inferred marginal demand. Do not assume equivalent listings have identical economics or fair value.
