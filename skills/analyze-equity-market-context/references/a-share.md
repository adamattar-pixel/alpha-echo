# A-share market context

Prefer Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges, CSRC, ministries, official statistics, issuer disclosures, and reproducible market datasets.

Evaluate index breadth, turnover, limit-up and consecutive-board structure, sector ladders, policy-to-industry diffusion, theme rotation, high/low switching, unlock pressure, and style leadership. Treat turnover as activity rather than precise flow. Do not turn a policy slogan or one limit-up into an industry earnings conclusion.
