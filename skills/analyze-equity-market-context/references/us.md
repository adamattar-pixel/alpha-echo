# US equity market context

Prefer SEC and issuer records, official agencies, exchanges, index providers, and scoped ETF, futures, or options datasets.

Evaluate breadth, equal-weight versus cap-weight leadership, growth/value and cyclical/defensive styles, earnings diffusion, rates and dollar transmission, theme lifecycle, valuation conditions, concentration, and crowding. Do not infer dealer positioning, institutional intent, or directional flow from volume alone.
